# FINAL FIT — Design Bible

## Pitch
A 3D anime-inspired fashion battle game. Walk a luxury cyber-anime store,
raid racks for drip under a round timer, build a fit on your customized adult
avatar, then present it on a runway in front of judges.

## Visual direction
- Anime-inspired, original characters only (style references are vibes, not copies).
- Luxury cyber-anime: dark glass, purple/pink gradients, neon trim, chrome, gold.
- Female presets support exaggerated curvy stylized proportions; male presets
  support lean model, athletic, muscular, anime-hero proportions. Androgynous
  high-fashion preset included. All avatars are adults, game-appropriate.
- The store must feel ALIVE: NPC shoppers drifting between racks, mannequins
  on pedestals in themed alcoves, a runway preview show looping with judges
  raising score paddles, signage and zone lighting everywhere.

## Round themes (phase 2 judging)
Opium Night · Old Money Yacht · Cyber Angel · Gothic Runway · Streetwear
Royalty · Red Carpet Villain · Y2K Mall Star · Luxury Airport Fit · Dark
Academia · Chrome Saint

## Judge categories (phase 2)
Theme accuracy, color harmony, silhouette, layering, accessory balance,
originality, risk level, overall cohesion. Rule-based over item tags/stats
first; no AI judging yet.

## Core loop
1. Main menu → 2. Character creator (preset, sliders, face, hair, skin)
→ 3. Fashion store (10:00 timer, browse racks, equip visible clothing)
→ 4. Outfit locks at 0:00 → 5. Runway cinematic → 6. Judging → 7. Results
→ back to store or menu.

## Hard "do not build" list
HTML game · 2D card-only game · single-file mockup · stick figures ·
colored-square-only clothing · static menus with no walking · multiplayer or
online features (not yet) · copyrighted anime characters.
